# Quality Gates

Use these checks before claiming a research draft workflow is complete.

## Universal Checks

- Original files were preserved.
- Preview copies were used for risky modifications.
- Final workbook opens through Excel/openpyxl.
- Key sheets exist or existing workbook structure was intentionally preserved.
- Source URLs, local files, page numbers, dates, and screenshots are traceable.
- Each key claim/data point has paired evidence or is listed in `待核验`.
- Screenshots visibly support their paired content.
- Blocked pages, paywalls, anti-bot pages, blank pages, estimates, and conflicting data are marked.
- Final file name, sheet names, headers, enum values, and reviewer notes are Chinese when delivering to a Chinese research workflow.

## Template Conversion Checks

- One-off company/task data was removed or clearly labeled as example content.
- Reusable sheet contracts and evidence fields are documented.
- Template workbook or contract does not overwrite the source file.

## New Draft Checks

- `数据总表` or equivalent structured sheet exists.
- `证据底稿` has content/evidence pairing.
- `待核验` records unresolved items.
- Data cutoff date and scope are clear when relevant.

## Existing Draft Update Checks

- Sheet order, names, formulas, screenshots, comments, and source notes outside the impacted area are preserved.
- Only impacted cells/charts/images/comments were changed.
- A preview copy was generated when practical.

## Chart Template Checks

- Template workbook was inspected.
- Chart families and palette/font conventions were recorded.
- Chart formatting was not tied to unrelated table/page layout.
- Chart XML or workbook inspection confirms expected style signals when feasible.

## Complex Scenario Checks

- The owning skill/workflow is identified when available.
- This skill only supplies draft contract and evidence rules.
- Handoff inputs and outputs are explicit.
- Scenario-specific quality gates are listed.
